## Quick Summary

Two-region (EU = Z1, Rest of World = Z2) MRIO-SFC model of circular economy transitions. 13 CE scenarios applied at ρ=0.2, t*=70 in a 100-period simulation. Three regime taxonomy: (1) Final demand shifts (1–6): low leverage; (2) Intermediate production shifts (7–13): strong ecological gains, contractionary. Core tension: ecological improvement ↔ macro-social contraction. EU–RoW asymmetries are a central finding. Scenario 14 (Construction) is excluded — misspecified.
